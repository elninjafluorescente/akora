Summary

Welcome to the perfect refuge where comfort and elegance meet. This charming apartment of modern, functional design invites you to live a unique experience in the heart of Madrid.

Description

The home comprises a bright double bedroom and a cosy single room, ideal for rest and disconnection. It offers two full bathrooms — one with a bathtub to unwind after a long day, the other with a contemporary shower — both designed for your comfort.

Space

A spacious, sophisticated dressing area gives generous room to organise your clothes with style. The climate is assured year-round thanks to air conditioning and three ceiling fans that keep the atmosphere fresh and pleasant in any season.

The living area is anchored by two flat-screen televisions, perfect for your series and films, and a fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances opening to an elegant dining zone. High-speed internet makes it ideal to work from home, study, or simply browse without interruption.

House Rules

We provide basic amenities for the first days of the stay; these aren't replenished. Any late or early check-in (between 21h and 9h) has an extra cost of 25€, paid in cash at the moment of check-in.

Cancellation Policy

If cancelled 0 to 14 days before arrival, the guest will be charged 100% of the total booking amount.
